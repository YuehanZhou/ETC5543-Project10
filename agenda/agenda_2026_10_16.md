# ETC5543 — Urban Sprawl and Biodiversity

## Meeting Agenda

**Date:** 16 October 2026

### 1. Progress since last meeting

- Completed the main development-pressure analysis using:
  - urban growth in hectares;
  - change in urban land share.

- Completed the habitat and biodiversity analysis using:
  - Habitat Value;
  - Golden Sun Moth records;
  - Growling Grass Frog records.

- Completed the BCS conservation-gap analysis.

- Created a master precinct-level dataset covering **105 PSP/MSA precincts**.

- Developed an ecological priority screening based on:
  - high development pressure;
  - high ecological value;
  - no BCS conservation coverage.

- The current screening identifies **15 ecological priority precincts**.

- Developed an interactive Shiny Priority Explorer to compare and rank all 105 precincts under different planning scenarios.

---

### 2. Current issues

- The current analysis is still mainly a **precinct-level ecological screening**.

- Compared with the original project brief, several areas are still relatively limited:
  - habitat fragmentation and connectivity are not directly analysed;
  - potential ecological corridors have not been identified;
  - statutory planning-overlay protection has not yet been included;
  - focal-species analysis is still mainly based on occurrence records.

- We would like to decide whether these areas should be added to strengthen the final analysis, while keeping the current work as the main foundation.

---

### 3. Topics for discussion

- Which additional analyses would provide the most value without making the project unnecessarily complicated?

- Should we add:
  - habitat fragmentation / connectivity analysis;
  - urban expansion versus high-value habitat overlap;
  - potential corridor-priority areas;
  - planning-overlay coverage;
  - sensitivity / robustness analysis?
