# ETC5543 — Urban Sprawl and Biodiversity

## Meeting Agenda

**Date:** 2 October 2026

### 1. Progress since last meeting

- PSP/MSA-level urban expansion analysis has been completed for the 105 precincts intersecting the five study LGAs.
- Two development-pressure indicators have been retained: absolute urban growth (`growth_ha`) and change in urban land share (`urban_share_change`).
- Precinct-level biodiversity indicators have been prepared, including mean Habitat Value, percentage of high-value habitat, BCS Conservation Area coverage, and focal-species occurrence records.
- Golden Sun Moth and Growling Grass Frog remain the two focal species used in the analysis.
- Development and biodiversity indicators have been combined into a single PSP/MSA-level master dataset containing 105 unique precincts.
- Focal-species occurrence has been converted into a presence/absence indicator for the preliminary priority analysis rather than using raw record counts.
- A preliminary rule-based priority analysis has been developed using development pressure, biodiversity importance and protection gap.
- Under the current preliminary rule, 15 precincts are identified as potential priority areas.

### 2. Topics for discussion

#### Priority-analysis framework

- Is it appropriate to define a priority precinct as an area with:
  - high development pressure,
  - high biodiversity importance,
  - and low or no conservation protection?

#### Development-pressure thresholds

- The current preliminary rule defines high development pressure as:
  - `growth_ha` above the median, or
  - `urban_share_change` above the median.
- Is using the median an appropriate threshold, or would another approach such as quartiles be preferable?
- Is it appropriate to retain both development indicators using an OR condition?

#### Biodiversity importance

- The current preliminary rule defines high biodiversity importance as:
  - `high_habitat_percent` above the median, or
  - presence of Golden Sun Moth or Growling Grass Frog.
- Is this combination appropriate?
- Is using species presence/absence preferable to using raw occurrence-record counts, given that record counts may reflect differences in survey effort?

#### Protection gap

- More than half of the study precincts have zero BCS Conservation Area coverage, so the overall median BCS coverage is zero.
- The current preliminary rule therefore defines the protection gap as `bcs_percent == 0`.
- Is zero BCS coverage an appropriate definition of a protection gap, or should low protection be defined using a different threshold?
- Should the protection-gap condition use zero BCS coverage only, or should precincts with low BCS coverage also be treated as having limited protection?

#### Final priority output

- Should the final analysis produce:
  - a rule-based priority classification,
  - a ranked list of precincts,
  - or both?
- Would a sensitivity analysis using alternative thresholds be useful to test whether the identified priority areas are robust?
